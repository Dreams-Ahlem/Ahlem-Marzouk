 Hello, my name is ![Ahlem](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahlem-marzouk/). With two master's degrees—in Mathematics and Big Data & Data Science—I have advanced expertise in predictive modeling, particularly as applied to time series and forecasting issues. With over 15 years of experience, I have designed, optimized, and deployed high-impact statistical and machine learning models.

🔬 Technical expertise:

Time series modeling:  SARIMAX, ETS, Prophet,  LSTM.

Predictive models: advanced regression, classification algorithms, churn detection.

Advanced analytics: customer segmentation, clustering (K-Means, DBSCAN), sentiment analysis (Deep Learning, LLM).


⚙️ Production & tools:

Design of end-to-end forecasting pipelines.

Deployment on Docker & Kubernetes.

Performance visualization and monitoring via Streamlit, Power BI, Tableau.

Development environments: Python, SQL, Spark, Git.

💼 Professional experience

I have led and completed several end-to-end projects, including the development of sales and demand forecasting solutions, the implementation of comprehensive pipelines and interactive dashboards to support strategic planning and dynamic pricing. These experiences have allowed me to develop an approach that combines scientific rigor and technical expertise.
Today, I remain open to new opportunities where I can put my data science skills to work on innovative, high-impact projects.

🎓 Academic background

At the same time, I have actively contributed to the academic world as a lecturer and supervisor at ESPRIT, a EUR-ACE®-accredited engineering school. There, I have mentored young data scientists on concrete projects in machine learning, deep learning, and time series. I have also published academic articles, demonstrating my ability to produce rigorous, structured, and recognized analyses.
These experiences have allowed me to strengthen my skills as a coach, mentor, and communicator, making complex concepts accessible to a variety of audiences.

I believe in the power of data to transform decision-making and create measurable value.
I am committed to making analyses accessible and sharing my knowledge so that each project combines scientific excellence, business impact, and expertise sharing.
